### What about **MTHL** specifically?

If you are representing sounds by consonants, **MTHL** can be surprisingly close because Germanic **d / ð / t** interchange historically within this family:

**M-Ð / D / T-L**

→ **mið- / mid- / mitt- + l**

So your **MTL / MTHL** pattern points quite naturally toward:

**middle → center → between → among / intermediate**

The reconstructed family is usually connected to Proto-Indo-European **\*medʰyo- “middle.”** [Wikisource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/An_Etymological_Dictionary_of_the_German_Language/Annotated/mitte?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

One particularly interesting form for your comparison is **German _Mittel_**, pronounced roughly **MIT-el**. So **MTL → MITTEL** is almost exact consonant-for-consonant.
