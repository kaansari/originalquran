Old Norse **mara**, Old High German **mara**, and Old English **mare/mære** all refer to a supernatural being believed to oppress sleepers—the source of English **nightmare**. **Old Norse mara → nightmare spirit**  
**Old High German mara → nightmare spirit**  
**Old English mare → nightmare spirit**

English **nightmare** originally meant the **night + mare**, i.e. the spirit thought to sit on a person's chest during sleep; only later did it come to mean a frightening dream. [Etymology Online](https://www.etymonline.com/word/nightmare?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

**MARA → nightmare/oppressive spirit**
